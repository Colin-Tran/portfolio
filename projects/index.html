Project 1: League of Legends -- An Objectives Analysis

For this project, I analyzed the importance of objectives in League of Legends. I used data from the Riot Games Esports to analyze the relationship between different objectives and objective control with winning. I found that teams that secure more objectives tend to win more often, highlighting the strategic importance of objectives in the game.

Project 3: SoCal Guesser

Built a convolutional neural network (CNN) to classify images by different Southern Californian (socal) cities, including San Diego, Los Angeles, SLO, Bakersfield, Riverside, and Anaheim. The model learns visual features from city landscapes and predicts correct location based on patterns the CNN identifiessuch as environment, architecture, and scenery.
